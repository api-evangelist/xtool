# xTool

> xTool is a digital-fabrication hardware brand (spun out of Makeblock, backed by HongShan) making CO2, fiber, and diode laser cutters and engravers, laser welders, and DTF printers, with the xTool Creative Space (XCS) desktop software driving its machines. xTool publishes no public developer API, SDK, or API documentation; its web surface is a Shopify storefront plus a support/academy portal and maker community.
